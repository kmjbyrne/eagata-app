Ghorkali Special

Hub Himalayan imitation.


Pressure cooker is fast but slow cooker results in better lamb bite.

Ingredients

  • 700 g lamb shoulder, cut into chunks.

  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee.

  • 1 tsp ground cumin.

  • 1 tsp turmeric.

  • 1 tbsp lemon juice.

  • 30g cashew nuts

Method

  1. Heat the oil or ghee in a heavy pot.

  2. Add water or stock, cover, and simmer.

    1. (Optional) Blend the gravy 

  3. Stir in the cream.
